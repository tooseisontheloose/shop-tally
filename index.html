Where prices come from

Prices without a tag come from Aldi UK's online catalogue, which lists the in-store price of each product. They're checked automatically every Friday morning and were last updated on . If the shelf says something different, tap the item and type the shelf price. Your price is used until the next Friday check.

Items you add yourself show a your price tag. The app remembers your price for that item next time.
